Article 50 Quick Check

Seven questions. You see the result in your browser, without giving us anything.

This is a screening aid, not a legal conclusion and not a miniature assessment. It cannot see your product. It points at whether something is worth reviewing.

1Do any of your AI systems have an EU connection?

Placed on the EU market, put into service in the EU, or their output used in the EU — wherever your company is based.

2For the systems with that EU connection, what is your role?

Provider: you develop it or place it on the market under your name. Deployer: you use a system in your own activity.

3Does a person interact directly with one of those AI systems?

A chatbot, an assistant, a voice agent, an in-product helper.

4Do those systems generate or manipulate text, image, audio or video?

Synthetic or edited content that you publish, send, or make available to someone.

5Do any of these specific situations apply to you?

These are named in Article 50 and are easy to overlook. Pick the closest one.

6Were any of those systems placed on the market or put into service before 2 August 2026?

This can change which date matters for the machine-readable marking obligation in Article 50(2).

7Today, do you disclose or mark this to the people affected?

This does not change whether anything applies to you — it only shows where a gap might be worth reviewing.
