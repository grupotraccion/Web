The problem it addresses

The EU AI Act's transparency obligations under Article 50 have applied since 2 August 2026. On 20 July 2026 the European Commission published the final version of its Guidelines on those obligations.

The guidance exists. Turning it into a per-system answer — which obligations reach this system, what has to be disclosed, to whom, and when — is a separate piece of work, and it is the one this assessment does.

Who it is for

  • Systems placed on the EU market or put into service in the EU.
  • Systems whose output is used in the EU, wherever the organisation sits.
  • Providers and deployers alike: Article 50 places obligations on both.
  • Cases involving direct interaction with a person, or generation or manipulation of text, image, audio or video, or deployer scenarios such as emotion recognition, biometric categorisation, deepfakes, or AI-generated text published to inform the public on matters of public interest.

Who it is not for

  • You want a certificate stating you are compliant — we do not issue one.
  • You need a legal opinion. That is a lawyer's work, not this product's.
  • You have no EU nexus at all: nothing placed on the EU market, nothing put into service there, and no output used there.

If you are unsure whether any of this reaches you, the free Quick Check is a better starting point than a purchase.

A note on timing

Systems already on the market before 2 August 2026 have until 2 December 2026 for the machine-readable marking obligation in Article 50(2). Content generated before 2 August 2026 does not require retroactive marking.

Whether that date is the relevant one for your systems is exactly the kind of question the assessment answers per system.
