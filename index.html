What you can verify before buying

  • Regulatory applicability calls cite the official source on which they are based — the Regulation text, the European Commission's Guidelines, or the Commission's FAQ.
  • A complete sample assessment is published, built around a fictional company, so you can judge the format before paying.
  • Assessment processing runs on our own server in Falkenstein, Germany, inside the European Economic Area.
  • Intake and report are deleted 90 days after delivery.
  • Conditional refund: if we cannot produce a useful assessment from what you send us, we refund in full.

How we work

  • Applicability is reasoned, not asserted. Each call is either LIKELY_APPLIES, LIKELY_NOT_APPLICABLE or REVIEW_REQUIRED, with the reasoning and the source behind it.
  • Open questions stay open. One section of the assessment is reserved for the points that need a lawyer, listed rather than smoothed over.
  • Scope is stated, not implied. What the product does not cover is written on the product page, before checkout.
