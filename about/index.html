About Grupo Tracción

Grupo Tracción SpA, established in Chile.

What we build

Products that take a body of requirements — a regulation, a process, a standard — and produce a per-case answer a team can act on: what applies here, what does not, and what evidence to keep.

The first of them is the Article 50 transparency readiness assessment. It is the only product currently on sale.

Method

  • Primary sources only for applicability. Calls rest on the Regulation text, the European Commission's Guidelines, or the Commission's FAQ.
  • Three outcomes, not two. Every call is LIKELY_APPLIES, LIKELY_NOT_APPLICABLE or REVIEW_REQUIRED. Forcing a binary answer where the source does not give one is how a document becomes confidently wrong.
  • Open questions are listed, not absorbed. A dedicated section names what needs a lawyer and why.
  • Automated where it is repeatable, reviewed where it is not. The pipeline runs unattended; the limits of what it can settle are stated in the document it produces.

Limits

Grupo Tracción does not provide legal advice and does not issue certificates of compliance. This assessment provides operational readiness and documentation support, not a legal opinion.

We are not a law firm and we do not hold ourselves out as one. Where a question requires legal judgement, the assessment says so instead of answering it.
